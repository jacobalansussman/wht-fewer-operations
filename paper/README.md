# The paper

**Revision 2, 22 October 2026.** The paper is the version of 11 October 2026 and is not changed in revision 2. Four things in it are superseded; the [README](../README.md) has the current text. (1) "The smallest constant that I found in print" should be read as the smallest constant of a count of the form c N log2 N plus a multiple of N: counts of a smaller order are in print, as the paper itself says in its abstract. (2) The figure 8.7625e-4 for my repository wht-power-saving-lean was its saving on 11 October; its README now gives 1.5699306e-3 for this transform. (3) The remarks on running time cover the one machine timed on 11 October; the measurements of 22 October are in [measurements/2026-10-22/](../measurements/2026-10-22/README.md). (4) Section 5 ("What has been checked, and how") says that nothing in count G is in Lean, and the abstract and the table of results in the introduction name Lean for count O only. Since revision 2, Theorem B is proved in Lean in the form "at most (3/4) N lg N + N/4 operations", in a model in which each operation is one multiply-add ±u ± c·v, over any commutative ring; the constants ±1 and ±2 are not part of the Lean statement. The 19 operations at 8 points are proved in Lean as well. See [VERIFY.md](../VERIFY.md).

- **What it is.** `explicit-wht-circuits.pdf` is the result of this repository written up as a paper: the circuits, the proofs of the counts, the tables, what was checked and how, what was measured about running time and about hardware, and what was read in the literature. `explicit-wht-circuits.tex` is its source.
- **Its status.** It is not peer reviewed and not submitted anywhere. AI agents (Anthropic's Claude) wrote it at my direction, and no person has yet checked its proofs line by line.
- **To build the PDF again.** In this folder, run `pdflatex explicit-wht-circuits.tex` twice. The source is one file and uses standard packages only; it was built with TeX Live 2026.
- **To check its numbers.** From the top folder of the repository, run `python3 -B paper/check.py 12 tools/circuits`. It recomputes every count and every construction of the paper up to 4,096 points, needs Python 3 with numpy, takes a few seconds and about 1 GB of memory, and ends with `ALL CHECKS PASSED.` It does not cover the measurements of Remark 10 and Section 8 (logic cells, clock estimates, timings); those have their own pages, [tools/circuits/fpga/README.md](../tools/circuits/fpga/README.md) and [tools/circuits/fast/wht56/results/ffht/README.md](../tools/circuits/fast/wht56/results/ffht/README.md).
- **To check two steps of the lower bound.** From the top folder, run `cc -O2 -o lowerbound_check paper/lowerbound_check.c && ./lowerbound_check`. It goes through every case of Steps 4 and 5 of the proof of Proposition 6 at 4 and 8 points, takes about ten seconds, and prints `the proof says at most 2: confirmed` twice. It is a check of those two steps at those two sizes, not of the proof.
