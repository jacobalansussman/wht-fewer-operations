# The code as run and as published

The programs in this folder are the programs that ran on 21-22 October 2026, with the changes listed here and no
others. The changes take out private details: paths on my computer, the launcher that started the jobs on the
rented machines and its slot names, and internal labels of the working folders. They also give the scripts plain
defaults where they had private ones. No line that computes, checks or times anything was changed. Three kinds of
change alter what a script does: the SIMDe check scripts now ask for the SIMDe and FFHT folders (SIMDE, FFHT)
instead of using folders on my computer, and the FPGA measurement tool has neutral defaults for its tool folders;
the Xilinx job skips its self-test unless SELFTEST=1, because the self-test reads designs that are not in this
repository; and two summary programs print a title without the internal label. In the "as run" column, private
parts are replaced by `<private path>`, `<launcher slot>`, `<private launcher>` or `<internal label>`.

This file was written by a program from the record of the copy: 93 distinct changes, 156 in all when the copies
of shared files (such as `az_pre.sh`, which each job folder carries) are counted.

## 3. What was taken out of the raw outputs

Raw outputs are published from each run's `out/` folder. Inside them:

- the quiet checks (`quiet.log`, and the same lines in `run.log` and `log.txt`) listed the busiest processes of
  the machine on lines starting `top:`; those lines are removed (they name the remote-access and notebook
  processes of the rented machine), and the verdict lines (QUIET or BUSY) are kept. Where a BUSY line named
  another process, its name and command line are replaced;
- the machine name of each rented container (in `machine.txt` and `run.log`) is replaced by `<container>`;
- the labels printed by the jobs themselves (for example a summary title) are replaced as in section 1;
- the GPU language-model runs keep their `res_*.json` files without the `outputs` arrays (16 MB of output
  tensors each), and of their build logs only the compiler (`ptxas`) lines of the 12N, 20N, 28N and 40N kernels;
  their `chk_*.json` files (output tensors only) are not published;
- the Xilinx run publishes its results, the logs of the published designs and the self-test rows of two of them;
- text files over 16 KB are stored compressed (`.gz`; 231 files), and each run's `asm/` folder as one `asm.tar.gz`.
