- **Logic cells** are those of the design alone: cells of the whole image less the harness. - **MHz** is nextpnr's maximum frequency after routing: median [lowest - highest] over the seeds. icetime's figure follows in the same form.
- **Latency** is in clock cycles from the input register to the registered output.
- **pK** means a register after every K-th adder level; p = p1.

# Second opinion: other FPGA families

Synthesis only (Yosys 0.69+274), the design module alone, no harness, no place and route, no timing. Commands: `synth_ice40`, `synth_ecp5`, `synth_xilinx -family xc7 -noclkbuf`, `synth_intel_alm -family cyclonev -noiopad -noclkbuf`. Counts are the cells yosys prints. ECP5: one CCU2C is two bits of carry chain and takes the place of two LUT4, so "LUT4 places" = LUT4 + 2 x CCU2C. Xilinx 7-series: one CARRY4 is four bits of carry chain; the LUTs in front of it are counted under LUT. Cyclone V: one MISTRAL_ALUT_ARITH per adder bit.
