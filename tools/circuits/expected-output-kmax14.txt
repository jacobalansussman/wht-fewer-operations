Small circuits for the Walsh-Hadamard transform H x on N = 2^k points.
Count (G): a gate is any a*u + b*v; multiplying by a constant is free.
Count (O): every addition, every subtraction and every multiplication by a constant costs one.
Everything below is computed in exact integers and fractions by Python.  It is not a proof.
